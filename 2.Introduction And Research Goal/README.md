Research Contributions
What This Study Adds to Knowledge
1. New Understanding of AI in Career Counseling
Most career theories talk about human counselors, teachers, and family members helping students choose careers. This study adds AI chatbots as a new type of "career helper." We want to see if AI can actually help students feel more confident about their career choices, just like a human counselor would.
2. Understanding HOW AI Helps (Not Just IF It Helps)
Many studies just ask "Does AI help?" We go deeper and ask "HOW does AI help?" We think AI helps in two ways: (a) it makes students explore more career options, and (b) it makes them feel more confident. This helps us understand the real benefit of AI, not just whether it works.
3. New Survey Questions for Measuring AI Use
Right now, there is no standard way to measure how students use AI for career help. We will create and test new survey questions that ask about: how often they use AI, what kinds of career questions they ask, how helpful they find AI advice, and how much they trust AI recommendations. Other researchers can use these questions in future studies.


How This Study Helps in Real Life
1. Better Career Services at Universities
Universities are starting to use AI tools in career centers, but they don't know the best way to do it. Our results will tell them: Should students use AI more? Should AI replace human counselors or work alongside them? Should universities teach students how to use AI for career planning?
2. Better AI Career Tools
Companies that build AI career chatbots will learn what features actually help students. For example: Should AI give lots of information or ask questions to make students think? Should AI give direct advice or help students explore options? Our study will show what works best.
3. Helping Students Feel More Confident
If we find that AI helps students feel more confident about career decisions, then career counselors can use AI as a tool to build student confidence, not just give information. This could help students who are shy or don't have access to human counselors.
How This Study Helps Other Researchers
1. New Survey Tool Everyone Can Use
We will test our new AI usage survey questions to make sure they work well (reliable and valid). Once published, other researchers can use these questions in their own studies instead of creating new ones from scratch.
2. Combining Old and New Methods
We use well-tested career surveys (that researchers have used for decades) together with new AI questions. This shows other researchers how to study new technology topics using established methods.
