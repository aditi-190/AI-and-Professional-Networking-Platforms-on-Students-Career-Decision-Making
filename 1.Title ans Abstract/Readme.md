The Influence of Generative AI-Assisted Career Guidance on Undergraduate Students' Career Decision-Making: The Mediating Roles of Career Exploration and Career Decision Self-Efficacy 

Abstract:
Generative AI tools such as ChatGPT are increasingly used by students for career advice, yet little is known about how this use relates to their career decisions. This study examines whether undergraduate students’ use of Generative AI for career guidance is associated with lower career decision-making difficulty among undergraduate students in Bangladesh. It also tests whether this relationship operates through two mediating mechanisms: career exploration and career decision self-efficacy. A cross-sectional survey will be conducted with 384 undergraduate students from different universities and majors, using a structured online questionnaire adapted from validated scales. The questionnaire measures (1) the use of Generative AI for career guidance, (2) career exploration, (3) career decision self-efficacy, and (4) career decision-making difficulty. The data will be analyzed using to test direct and indirect effects. The findings are expected to offer insights for universities and career counselors on integrating Generative AI tools into career counseling and on helping students use them more effectively for career planning.
